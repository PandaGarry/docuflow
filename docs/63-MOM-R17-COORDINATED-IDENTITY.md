## Actual asset and scope

Load the **actual saved R16 binary**, verify its SHA and archived receipt, edit
the genuine original Rain head. No replacement head, canonical-image texture
projection, generated image, screen-space repaint or feature warping.

- Oral region: smooth ~4.3 mm lift, localized lip retreat, slightly narrower and
  less vertically exaggerated vermilion, relaxed mouth corners. Gingiva/tongue
  get the corresponding smooth oral mapping.
- Correctives: reevaluate the spatial sculpt on each of the25 original blocks.
  Preserve topology, weights, key names, drivers and rig rest. Coordinate deltas
  are deliberately transported, **not claimed byte-identical**. Strict source-
  to-candidate orientation checks remain enabled on EVERY original block.
- Eyes: contract physical pupil/iris surface in XZ with a smoothly held sclera
  boundary. Retain original cornea coordinates, UVs and sampled eye texture.
  Lighter blue-gray iris response and resized/raised original catchlight mesh;
  no globe-depth/cornea scaling or fake iris image projection.
- Nose: bounded round guide/fairing; smooth naris rim + inner-wall contraction.
  Apex largely held, not a new protruding replacement nose.
- Brows: raise inner region more than outer, retain original strip and fibers.
- Skin: newly authored2048px packed linear EXR albedo/PBR maps on the ORIGINAL
  UV layout. Rosy nasal/cheek pigment, coral lips, separate roughness/coat and
  controlled SSS/specular response. Shader pigment is calibrated under the
  explicit stage; nominal palette is not a guarantee of matching display RGB.
- Hair: **25 individually rounded contour-flow front tresses +11 rear waves**,
  fine native surface fibers and subtle longitudinal detail. Closed independent
  volumes, not a broad shell, crown cap or high-contrast painted stripe pattern.
  Manual canonical XZ contours guide flow; Y/depth are independently authored.
  Actual head BVH supplies smooth scalp clearance; this is NOT a follicle/root
  dynamics certificate. Hair is rigidly attached to original `DEF-Head`.
- R16 clothes are unchanged and visibly unfinished. Head/hair review crops are
  explicitly labeled; intact raw portraits also show the existing wardrobe.

## Evidence and technical versus artistic acceptance

Full scope: new front/three-quarter/profile portraits, unobscured face,3clay
views, actual R16 bare face under the SAME new lighting, saved neutral and
separate-process reopened neutral. Original head/eyes/UV/material/rest are
checked against saved receipts; packed textures and reopen image agreement are
verified. These checks do not establish likeness, professional art quality,
complete facial corrective migration, blink closure or usable production acting.

Boards use uniform eye-anchor registration only. No feature-local fitting,
retouch, canonical texture sampling, image generation or numerical likeness
score. The different lighting from historical R16 is explicit; the additional
held-light R16 baseline prevents attributing lighting-only effects to sculpture.

Known blink/lash visual failure, full facial-rest/corrective migration,
acting/lip-sync, shoulder hair dynamics and clothing are **not certified**.
No user/art approval, production swap or PR merge is inferred.
