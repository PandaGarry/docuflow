  folds remain readable; not hidden with hair or a camera change.

nose. Lip contour and forward projection are still unlike the reference; the
current smile is not final. Full eye/lid expression, hair/frame, body/clothes,
acting and rest migration remain unfinished. Known blink/lash failure remains
unresolved. No exact likeness/cinema-quality/user/production approval claimed.
